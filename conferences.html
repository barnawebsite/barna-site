36th Annual Conference — 2026

Friday 5 June 2026 · Bush House, King's College London

A full day across airway management, paediatric practice, research careers and education, opening with the 2025 Difficult Airway Society guidelines and closing on current topics in airway management. The programme also carried a dedicated abstracts session showcasing work from members.

35th Annual Conference & Exhibition — 2025

Friday 25 April 2025 · St Thomas' Hospital, London

A programme combining managerial, clinical, education and quality assurance content, designed to cover the needs of all grades across anaesthetic and PACU practice. Delegate rates were £55 for members, £95 for non-members and £20 for student members.

CPD

One hour of CPD was awarded for each session, with sessions comprising 45 minutes of participatory learning followed by discussion. Time spent in participatory learning, plus any further reading delegates went on to do, counted towards the 35 hours of continuing professional development required for NMC revalidation.

34th Annual Conference — 2024

Earlier Conferences

The Annual Conference ran in Birmingham from 1987, moved to Brighton in 2003 and to London in 2008. These are the most recent meetings before the current run.

2020–2023 No Annual Conference — Covid-19 and its aftermath

The conference scheduled for 19 June 2020 at the Birmingham and Midland Institute was cancelled, then rescheduled for 18 June 2021 and cancelled again. Virtual presentations were offered in their place. An online autumn study day was held on Friday 3 November 2023 in place of a conference, before the Annual Conference returned in 2024.

Friday 7 June 2019 33rd Annual Conference — Greenwich University, Queen Anne Building

The last Annual Conference before the pandemic.

Friday 22 June 2018 32nd Annual Conference — Greenwich University, Queen Anne Building

The second of three consecutive conferences held at Greenwich.

Friday 16 June 2017 31st Annual Conference — Greenwich University, Queen Anne Building

The first BARNA conference at the Queen Anne Building, Greenwich.

13–16 May 2016 IFNA Congress — Scottish Exhibition and Conference Centre, Glasgow

BARNA hosted the International Federation of Nurse Anesthetists congress in Glasgow, with delegates from all over the world. BARNA has been the UK representative body for IFNA since 1995.
