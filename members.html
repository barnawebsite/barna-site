Upcoming Partner Webinar

From ICPAN, the International Collaboration of PeriAnaesthesia Nurses — one of the bodies BARNA helped found, and to which BARNA is the official UK representative.

ICPAN WEBINAR

mtND4 11232 Mutation: What Do We Know So Far? The Current Situation in Spain

Saturday 10 October 2026 · two sessions: 12:00 noon UK time in English, 4:00pm UK time in Spanish

Elena Méndez Martínez, MD — Consultant Anaesthetist and Intensivist, University Hospital Central of Asturias, and lead of the mtND4 Working Group.

Following reports in summer 2025 of serious adverse events in patients of Venezuelan ancestry given general anaesthesia with halogenated agents, the Spanish Society of Anaesthesiology and Resuscitation set up a working group on the mutation. This session covers what the evidence shows so far and how the safety recommendations are developing.
