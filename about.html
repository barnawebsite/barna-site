Mission & Values

Our Mission: To support all perioperative professionals in delivering the highest standards of care in patients undergoing anaesthesia in any setting.

Our Vision: Excellence in care through education, audit and research.

Our Values: To value everyone who contributes to our work. To expect and encourage the highest possible standards.

BARNA aims to:

  • Foster communication and collaboration among professional partners
  • Maximise opportunities for professional development
  • Develop learning and teaching resources
  • Promote research and evidence-based, quality nursing practice

Governance

BARNA is a Registered Charity (1039150). It is managed by a Board of Trustees elected from the membership. The AGM is held at the Annual Conference, where committee members are elected. Members may request copies of AGM minutes and the Financial Report.

BARNA is the official UK representative to IFNA and ICPAN — on joining, all members automatically become members of both. BARNA elects a UK representative to each organisation's governing board.

Get Involved

BARNA is run by a small team of dedicated professionals, and we always need more help developing our services and reaching new members. If you'd like to get involved, there are many ways to contribute: reviewing or developing education resources, sharing local knowledge (hospitals, contacts, venues), helping with events, or lending IT, admin, or design skills.

Get in touch to find out more: connectwithbarna@gmail.com.

History

BARNA was founded in 1987 with a specific focus on anaesthesia and recovery nurses in Birmingham. For over 30 years BARNA has hosted annual conferences, study days and discussion forums.
