This resource is directed towards care of the adult airway in the PACU. As can be seen in the above table, the subject area has been divided into six sections. The focus is always on the airway. Supplementary notes and the Trainee Workbook combine information about all six elements. Posters focus on specific areas.

We hope that you will find this resource useful. We welcome any of your comments and ideas for improvement. Also if you would like to join the BARNA Education Team to help develop more resources please contact connectwithbarna@googlemail.com.
